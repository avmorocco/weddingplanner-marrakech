**Privacy Policy**

### 1. Commitment to Discretion

At Marrakech Wedding Planner, discretion and privacy protection for our couples and their guests are absolute priorities. We apply rigorous protocols in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on personal data protection.

### 2. Data Collection & Processing

Data collected via our inquiry forms (names, emails, phone numbers, event details, estimated budget) is strictly reserved for our private concierge team to craft your bespoke proposals. It is never sold, leased, or disclosed to commercial third parties.

### 3. Right of Access & Deletion

You retain permanent rights of access, correction, and deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact our team via email at: ocrewedding.marrakech@gmail.com.
