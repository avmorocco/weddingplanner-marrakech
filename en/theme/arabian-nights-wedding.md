The Theme's Narrative

## Royal Opulence & Oriental Splendor

Conceived for couples who desire a majestic, theatrical experience for their guests, this theme pays tribute to the grandeur of Morocco's historic dynasties.

Within grand royal salons or palace gardens of the Palmeraie, we curate an enchanting fantasy: monumental brass chandeliers, arches draped in gold-embroidered velvet and silk, shimmering silverware, and cascades of red roses and rare orchids.

The entrance of the newlyweds carried in an ornate carved silver amariya, heralded by Sufi polyphonic chants and enthusiastic applause, crowns an unforgettable night.
