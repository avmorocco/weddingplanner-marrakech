The Theme's Narrative

## Mineral Grandeur & Starlit Skies

Just 40 minutes from the bustle of Marrakech, the Agafay stone desert provides a striking contrast: a moonscape dipped in gold against the snowy High Atlas peaks.

Here we erect ephemeral haute-couture camps inspired by royal caravans of old: white caidal pavilions, handwoven wool carpets, pathways lined with 1,200 pierced lanterns, and a grand bonfire under the Milky Way.

As the sun slips behind the dunes in an explosion of ochre and purple, the vast expanse transforms into a boundless celestial ballroom.
