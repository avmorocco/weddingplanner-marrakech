The Theme's Narrative

## Botanical Poetry & Timeless Sanctuary

Behind the carved cedar doors of the Medina alleys lie hidden sanctuaries of peace, where nature and Hispano-Moorish architecture merge.

The Secret Garden theme speaks to romantic souls who treasure intimacy and exquisite details. We adorn the central courtyard with thousands of fresh rose petals floating on the pool, suspended eucalyptus canopies, and candles along the intricate wooden balconies.

Round tables draped in delicate fabrics host fine gold-trimmed china, softly illuminated by the flickering light of Moroccan brass lanterns.
