Rustic Bohemian

The poetic union of raw natural elements and wild Moroccan flora, beneath the century-old canopies of an olive grove in the Palmeraie.

Home • Weddings • Rustic Bohemian
The Theme's Narrative

The Warmth of Earth & Elements

The Rustic Bohemian theme reinterprets European country chic with the tactile sensuality of handcrafted Berber textures.

Picture raw oak banquet tables set directly upon the lawn, runners of natural ecru cotton gauze, and hand-woven olive garlands. Wispy pampas grass sways in the evening breeze, while dozens of rattan lanterns cast an amber glow.

An organic, heartfelt, and delightfully unconventional celebration, perfect for couples seeking relaxed sophistication and a return to authenticity.
